# Health Insurance and Out-of-Pocket Health Spending in Vietnam

> **Status: working project, not peer reviewed.** Results are preliminary and are not summarised here; see the manuscripts in the repository.

Health Economics course project (UEH) on the effect of health insurance participation on household out-of-pocket health expenditure in Vietnam. Contents: Stata do-files and logs (VHLSS and TVSEP workflows), two LaTeX manuscript versions, and group presentation slides.

**Author:** Truong Nhat Duy (Truong Nhat Duy), University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City (UEH).

**Period:** Dec 2025 - Mar 2026 (Health Economics course, UEH). Dates are taken from file timestamps.

## Data availability

Survey microdata (`.dta`/`.sav`) are excluded. VHLSS must be obtained from the General Statistics Office of Vietnam under its terms of use; TVSEP from its data provider. Literature PDFs and other groups' submissions are not included. Replicating the analysis requires obtaining the original data from the providers.
